## 💎 What this App Does

### 💸 Simple Expense Tracking
* **Add Expenses:** Easily type in a title, cost, and pick a custom date for any transaction.
* **Smart ID Helper:** Automatically creates a unique ID for every new item behind the scenes so your data stays organized.
* **Quick Delete:** Tap the trash icon to instantly remove any expense from your list and refresh the screen.

### 📊 Weekly Spending Graph
* **7-Day History:** The app automatically filters your list to look at only your last 7 days of spending.
* **Dynamic Bars:** Calculates your total spending and automatically grows or shrinks the graph bars to show which days you spent the most.
* **Safe Coding:** Built-in safeguards stop the app from crashing if you haven't spent any money yet.

### 📱 Full Phone Support
* **Screen Rotation:** Turn your phone sideways, and the app automatically adjusts! It adds a toggle switch so you can choose to see the chart or the list.
* **Keyboard Friendly:** The input form automatically rolls up when your keyboard appears so it never blocks what you are typing.
* **Fast Performance:** Uses a smart list builder (`ListView.builder`) so the app stays fast and smooth, no matter how many expenses you add.
